## Why it matters for TickForge
Background merging (compaction) can cause latency spikes on the write path. Our plan is to slow compaction down when the order queue is busy. SILK is the closest prior work, so our version must explain how it differs. The database has one plain size-tiered compaction (`storage/src/database.rs`), but nothing slows it down when the queue is busy: that part is not built, and no latency spike has been measured.
